# Campus Customs Design Notes

## Visual direction

The site now uses a cohesive Yale-inspired system: deep ink navy for the storefront shell, warm paper for browsing and purchase decisions, Yale blue for supporting accents, and a bright heritage yellow for calls to action. DM Sans handles interface text, DM Mono labels metadata, and Playfair Display adds editorial warmth to large headlines.

## Major changes

- Rebuilt the navigation, hero, typography scale, spacing, buttons, and responsive breakpoints as one consistent design system.
- Added a seasonal “The Edit” featured-merchandise section to the home page so shoppers see products immediately and have clear paths into the collection.
- Redesigned product cards with editorial image framing, stronger price hierarchy, larger hover treatment, and clearer spacing. Product browsing now sits on a warm paper canvas for contrast.
- Redesigned product detail pages around the purchase decision: larger imagery, stronger product hierarchy, readable descriptions, visible stock-by-size, and a prominent add-to-bag action.
- Integrated the chatbot into the storefront with a branded navy panel, status indicator, compact message bubbles, product-result cards, loading feedback, and a responsive mobile layout.
- Brought authentication into the same visual language with a focused card, subtle radial lighting, clear input focus states, and consistent calls to action.
- Added restrained entrance, hover, lift, and chat-panel motion to make the experience feel responsive without slowing down shopping.

These choices make the brand feel more considered, help visitors understand where to shop immediately, and keep product discovery, evaluation, and conversation visually connected.
